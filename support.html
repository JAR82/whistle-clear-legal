Common questions

I can't restore my Remove Ads purchase.
Open the map screen and tap "Restore purchases" while signed into the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

My season progress didn't carry over to a new device.
Whistle Clear's week/zone progress and best score are stored locally on-device — they aren't currently synced across devices.

How does Continue work?
When a drive ends, you can watch a rewarded ad or spend the Continue purchase to keep playing the same drive instead of starting over.
